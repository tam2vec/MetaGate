              Security & RBAC
              
              
              
                
              Read-only defaultPredicate evaluates DataHub metadata without sending mutations unless write-back is explicitly configured.
              
                
              Server-side tokenDataHub tokens stay in the private Predicate API, not in the public page or browser panel.
              
                
              Separated rolesRequester asks for an AI action, Predicate evaluates policy, metadata owner repairs evidence, approver enables write-back.
              
                
              Mutation gateLive write-back requires deployment-approved GraphQL mutation documents.
